The Pyramids Of Egypt

The Pyramids of Giza, located on the outskirts of Cairo, Egypt, stand as the lastremaining Wonder of the Ancient World. Constructed during the 4th Dynasty of the Old Kingdom (c. 2580–2560 BC), these architectural marvels served as eternal tombs for the pharaohs Khufu, Khafre, and Menkaure. The Great Pyramid of Khufu is the largest, originally rising to 146.6 meters. It was the world's tallest man-made structure for over 3,800 years, built from approximately 2.3 million limestone and granite blocks. Its precision remains a mystery, with the sides aligned almost perfectly to the cardinal points of the compass. Adjacent to the pyramids lies the Great Sphinx, a limestone statue with a lion's body and a pharaoh's head, believed to represent Khafre. The site also includes smaller queen’s pyramids, causeways, and a recently excavated workers' village, which suggests the labor was performed by skilled, paid workers rather than slaves. Today, the Giza Plateau is a UNESCO World Heritage site. Visitors can explore the burial chambers, visit the nearby Grand Egyptian Museum, and witness the nightly Sound and Light show that illuminates these ancient giants, bridging the gap between modern civilization and the dawn of history.

The Great Pyramid of Giza (Khufu)

The oldest and largest, originally standing 146.6 meters (481 ft) tall. It was the world's tallest man-made structure for over 3,800 years and is the only surviving member of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World

The Pyramid of Khafre

Appears taller than the Great Pyramid because it sits on higher ground; it is easily identified by the remaining white limestone casing at its peak.

The Pyramid of Menkaure

The smallest of the main three. Recent 2026 research using radar and ultrasound has identified hidden air-filled voids behind its eastern face, suggesting undiscovered chambers or entrances.

Do you know?

Despite centuries of study, the pyramids continue to reveal new "secrets" through advanced technology: The "Big Void": Using muon tomography (scanning with cosmic particles), scientists discovered a massive, unexplored void at least 30 meters long situated above the Grand Gallery in the Great Pyramid. Hidden Chambers in Menkaure: In 2026, researchers using radar found previously unknown air-filled voids behind the eastern face of the Pyramid of Menkaure, hinting at undiscovered chambers or passages [Previous Turn]. Missing Mummies: Although built as royal tombs, no mummies have ever been found inside the Great Pyramid. They were likely looted in antiquity, but some speculate the true burial chambers remain hidden.
