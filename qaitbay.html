Qaitbay Citadel




Alexandria's Historic Fortress on the Mediterranean

About the Citadel

Qaitbay Citadel is a 15th-century fortress in Alexandria, Egypt. It was built by Sultan Al-Ashraf Qaitbay between 1477 and 1479 AD. The citadel stands on the site of the famous Lighthouse of Alexandria, one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World.

Today, it is one of Egypt's most important landmarks and houses a naval museum. Visitors can enjoy stunning views of the Mediterranean Sea.

Did You Know?

The citadel was built using stones from the ancient Lighthouse of Alexandria.

It survived a British bombardment in 1882.

The fortress has a small mosque inside that is still used today.

It is one of the best-preserved Mamluk fortresses in the Mediterranean.

You can see the entire Alexandria coastline from its walls.
