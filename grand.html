Grand Egyptian Museum

The world's largest archaeological museum dedicated to one civilization.

About the Museum

The Grand Egyptian Museum is one of the most important cultural landmarks in Egypt, located near the Pyramids of Giza.

Historical Background

The Grand Egyptian Museum project was launched to preserve and display Egypt's rich ancient heritage.

It was designed to become the largest museum in the world dedicated to ancient Egyptian civilization.

The museum includes thousands of artifacts, including treasures of King Tutankhamun.
