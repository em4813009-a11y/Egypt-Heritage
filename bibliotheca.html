About The library

The Alexandria Library in the past was really a legend. They built it for a long time in the era of the Ptolemies, meaning around the third century BC, and some people say that its idea was due to Alexander the Great himself. It was the largest library in the ancient world, full of hundreds of thousands of papyrus rolls (they said it would reach 700,000 books or more). Scientists from every part were going to read, research and write: Like Euclid, who laid the foundation of engineering, Archimedes, and others. But as you know, the fire that occurred in 48 BC (when Julius Caesar was fighting) destroyed a large part of it, and with time it completely disappeared. It was a beacon of true knowledge, not just a library, this was the center of great minds.
